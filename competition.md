# Competition
Our competition falls into two major catagories:
1) Physical Banks
2) Purely Online Banks

## Physical Banks
Major banks such as TD bank or RBC have their own websites/apps to do online banking to supplement their physical locations. They allow their users to do the following:
- check their balances of their various accounts (chequing, savings, etc.)
- send money (payees/e-transfer)
- deposit cheques

That is the basic functionality of their online banking websites/apps, but each specific one may have more features.

## Purely Online Banks
Online Only banks such as Tangarine focuses on no fee, low-cost banking. They allow their users to do the following:
- Open balance account (chequing, savings)
- send money (payees/e-transfer)
- Invest their money
- Open credit cards
- Take loans and morgages
- Money transfer between native and external bank accounts.

## Our Difference
Our product has the same basic functionality as the two specified above, with a focus on:
- Management of reoccuring payments
  - The management of reoccuring payments such as subscriptions, bills and loans is done by allocating money into a special account. By doing this, money is taken from this account so that it is always paid on time, as long as the remaining allocated money can cover it. Otherwise, the payment will automatically be prevented. 
- Sharing accounts and expenses
  - Users can make shared accounts where money can be pooled together and then used.
- Allowing limited overdrafting
- Managing multiple currency accounts
  - Allow conversion between currencies at current rate.
