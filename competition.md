Competition falls into two major catagories:
1) Major Banks
2) Payment Platforms

## Major Banks
Major banks such as TD bank or RBC have their own websites/apps to do online banking. They allow their users to do the following:
- check their balances of their various accounts (chequing, savings, etc.)
- send money (payees or other bank accounts)
- deposit cheques

That is the basic functionality of their online banking websites/apps, but each specific one may have more features.

## Payment Platforms
Payment Platforms such as Paypal and Cashapp act as a middleman for payment. Money is sent to these payment platforms and then is stored in an account. This money can then be transfered between paypal accounts, or deposited into a bank account.

Those platforms also include as a payment option.
