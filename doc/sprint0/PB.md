# Product Backlog
- As a user, I want to set up custom notifications for low balances or large transactions, so I can stay informed and avoid overdraft fees.
- As a user, I want to access a portion of my paycheck early, so I can manage cash flow better and cover unexpected expenses.
- As a loan applicant, I want a visual dashboard that tracks my loan status, payment history, and remaining balance, so I can easily monitor my progress toward loan payment.
- As a user, I want the ability to temporarily freeze my debit or credit card through the app, so I can protect my funds in case of theft or loss.
- As a user, I want the option to donate to charitable causes directly from my account, with the bank matching a portion of my donations, so I can support meaningful causes.
- As a user, I want to apply for and manage peer-to-peer loans through the platform, so I can either lend money to others and earn interest or access small loans quickly.
- As a loan applicant, I want to check my loan eligibility and receive a predicted approval percentage, so I can make informed decisions before applying.
- As a user, I want to split bills with my friends and family directly from the app, so I can easily manage shared expenses.
- As a user, I want to receive real-time notifications for any suspicious activities on my account, so I can take immediate action if fraud occurs.
- As a user, I want to track all my monthly subscription payments in one place, so I can avoid unexpected charges and manage my finances better.
- As a user, I want the ability to set flexible loan repayment schedules, such as weekly or bi-weekly payments, so I can match repayments with my income cycle.
- As a user, I want to set up joint savings goals with my family members, so we can all contribute towards shared financial objectives like a vacation or a down payment.
- As a user, I want to manage my foreign currency accounts (e.g., CAD, USD, EUR) within the app, so I can easily switch between currencies when traveling or making international purchases.
- As a user, I want to generate monthly expense reports that summarize my spending categories, so I can understand where my money goes and adjust my budget.
- As a user, I want to set up recurring payments for bills, loans, and subscriptions, so I can automate my finances and avoid missed payments. Or only for particular months.
- As a user, I want to get notified about new promotions and interest rates for savings accounts or loans, so I can take advantage of better financial products.
- As a user, I want to track my spending limits for specific categories (e.g., groceries, dining), so I can stay within my budget and reduce unnecessary expenses.
- As a user, I want to view real-time exchange rates within the app when converting currencies, so I can make informed decisions when traveling or making international transactions.
- As a user, I want to set savings goals within the app and track my progress, so I can stay motivated to reach my financial targets (e.g., saving for a car or vacation).
- As a user, I want to monitor my credit score in real-time through the app, so I can understand how my financial habits affect my credit health.

# Samantha – Young Professional (Persona 1)

Needs: Manage personal finances, save for a condo, explore investment opportunities, and stay organized with subscriptions.

What She Gets:
- Automatic savings through micro-savings and personalized investment suggestions.
- Subscription tracking and bill splitting for easy financial management with friends and family.
- Notifications for her balance and real-time fraud alerts for increased security.
- Taking credit before receiving the pay check

# John – Small Business Owner (Persona 2)

Needs: Manage multi-currency accounts, handle business expenses, and access flexible loan options for business growth.

What He Gets:
- Multi-currency account management for easy travel and business expenses abroad.
- Access to flexible loan repayment schedules and loan health dashboards.
- Real-time fraud alerts and card freezing features for enhanced security on the go.
- Easy-to-use account management with customer support chat to resolve issues quickly.

# Emily – University Student (Persona 3)

Needs: Track her monthly expenses, manage subscriptions, split bills with roommates, and improve her credit score.

What She Gets:
- Subscription management and bill splitting features to help manage shared expenses with her friends.
- Notifications about upcoming bills and balance alerts to avoid overdraft fees.
- Real-time credit score monitoring and tips to build her credit score responsibly.
- Personalized savings advice and tools for setting short-term financial goals.
