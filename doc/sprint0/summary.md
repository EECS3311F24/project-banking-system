# Project Objective
The project aims to develop a modern banking application that empowers users to manage their finances effortlessly while promoting financial well-being. By focusing on ease of use, automation, and personalized financial tools, the app will provide an intuitive interface that simplifies daily account management, enhances loan tracking, and supports smart financial decisions. Through a combination of flexible loan management features, budget tracking, savings goals, and automated payments, the app will help users streamline their financial activities, encouraging responsible spending and ensuring long-term financial stability.
## Key Personas
### Samantha – Young Professional (Persona 1)

Needs: Manage personal finances, save for a condo, explore investment opportunities, and stay organized with subscriptions.

What She Gets:
- Automatic savings through micro-savings and personalized investment suggestions.
- Subscription tracking and bill splitting for easy financial management with friends and family.
- Notifications for her balance and real-time fraud alerts for increased security.
- Taking credit before receiving the pay check

### John – Small Business Owner (Persona 2)

Needs: Manage multi-currency accounts, handle business expenses, and access flexible loan options for business growth.

What He Gets:
- Multi-currency account management for easy travel and business expenses abroad.
- Access to flexible loan repayment schedules and loan health dashboards.
- Real-time fraud alerts and card freezing features for enhanced security on the go.
- Easy-to-use account management with customer support chat to resolve issues quickly.

### Emily – University Student (Persona 3)
Needs: Track her monthly expenses, manage subscriptions, split bills with roommates, and improve her credit score.

What She Gets:
- Subscription management and bill splitting features to help manage shared expenses with her friends.
- Notifications about upcoming bills and balance alerts to avoid overdraft fees.
- Real-time credit score monitoring and tips to build her credit score responsibly.
- Personalized savings advice and tools for setting short-term financial goals.

## Key Scenarios
### Scenario 1 – Samantha

Samantha logs into the banking app. She immediately sees her current balance and recent transactions. She notices a few subscription payments she forgot about and uses the subscription tracker feature to cancel unnecessary ones, and automate some only for few months. Samantha sets up a savings goal for a condo down payment and activates the micro-savings feature to automatically save small amounts. She also receives investment suggestions that align with her risk profile, helping her explore early investment options.
### Scenario 2 – John

John travels frequently for work and needs access to different currencies. Using the banking app, he can view his multi-currency accounts and switch between CAD, USD and EUR seamlessly. He receives a notification about a recent large transaction while traveling and uses the card freezing feature to secure his account until the issue is resolved. Later, he checks his loan health dashboard to monitor the progress of his small business loan, viewing his upcoming payments and remaining balance.
### Scenario 3 – Emily

Emily is a university student living with roommates, and they often share bills. She uses the bill-splitting feature to divide their rent and utility payments, making it easy to track who owes what. Emily also sets up alerts for her account balance and receives a notification when her funds drop below a certain threshold, helping her avoid overdrafts. She regularly checks her credit score using the app’s credit monitoring tool.


## Key Design Principles
**User-Centric Design**: The banking application is designed with a strong focus on ease of use and user satisfaction. It provides easy financial insights, simple navigation, and relevant features that meet the needs of different user personas.

**Automation for Efficiency**: The application integrates automation where possible to reduce manual tasks for users. This includes automatic transfers, loan repayment scheduling, and real-time updates, ensuring that users can manage their finances with minimal effort.

**Accessibility and Flexibility**: The app offers flexible features like multi-currency account management, customizable alerts, and personalized savings tools, ensuring it can meet the diverse financial needs of young professionals, small business owners, and students alike.

**Empower Financial Wellness**: The core principle behind the app is to empower users with the tools and knowledge to make better financial decisions. From tracking expenses to providing financial literacy through educational content, the goal is to improve users’ financial health.
